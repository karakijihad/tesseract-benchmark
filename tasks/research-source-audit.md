# Example: research source audit

## Reference mode

`discovery`

## End goal

Produce a short decision brief that answers a defined question using current, authoritative sources and makes every important claim traceable.

## Contestant brief

Given a decision question and a deadline, research the answer using public sources. Produce `answer.md` with a concise recommendation, a claim table, exact source URLs, access dates, a note about conflicting evidence, and a section called `Uncertainty`. Do not cite a search result without opening the source. Do not present an inference as a source statement.

## Minimal reference

Supply only the question, the audience, the decision deadline, and any exclusions. Do not supply the source list.

## Validator

- Required claims are present.
- Every material claim has at least one source URL.
- URLs resolve and the cited page supports the claim.
- The answer distinguishes source fact, inference, and recommendation.
- The answer includes uncertainty and does not claim more precision than the sources support.
- The report is within the requested length.

## What it measures

Research planning, source selection, freshness, citation discipline, synthesis, uncertainty, and resistance to confident unsupported answers.
