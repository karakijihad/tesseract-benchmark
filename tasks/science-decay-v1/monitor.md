# Monitor notes

The validator checks the files, not the final message. It compares the half-life and background against the values used to generate the data, and checks that the stated uncertainties are in a believable range. A fit that leaves out the background gives a half-life that is clearly too long and fails.

A person reviews the report for how well the method is explained and whether the cited sources are relevant to the method. The validator only checks that two distinct links are present.
