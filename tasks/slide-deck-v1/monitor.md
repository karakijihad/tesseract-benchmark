# Monitor notes

The validator opens `deck.pptx` with python-pptx and checks structure only: slide count, a title on the first slide, no empty slide, speaker notes, a native chart or table whose numbers match the supplied table, a list of required facts found in slide text or notes, and a closing slide that lists the sources.

It does not judge how the deck looks. A person must open the deck and judge visual quality: layout, readable text sizes, text that fits its boxes, a consistent style, and whether the chart or table is clear. Record that judgement in the quality score, not in the completion score.
