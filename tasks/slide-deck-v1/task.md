# Slide deck from supplied material

## Context

Your folder contains `source.md`, a short factual document about a famous bridge. It has a few sections of prose and one small table. Read all of it before you start.

## End goal

A slide deck, `deck.pptx`, that a presenter could use to give a short talk on the bridge to a general audience, built only from the material in `source.md`.

## Requirements

1. Save the deck as `deck.pptx`, a real PowerPoint file, in your folder.
2. The deck has between 8 and 12 slides.
3. The first slide is a title slide with a title.
4. Every slide has something on it. No slide may be empty.
5. Every slide except the first and the last has speaker notes that say what the presenter would tell the audience.
6. The deck contains at least one native PowerPoint chart or table built from the table in `source.md`. It must be an editable chart or table, not a picture of one, and its numbers must match the source.
7. The deck uses the facts in `source.md` accurately: the name and location of the bridge, the key dates, the main dimensions, the cost, how it was built and how it compares with other bridges. Do not add facts that are not in the source.
8. The last slide is titled Sources and lists the sources named at the end of `source.md`.

## Constraints

Use any tools you normally use to make the file. Work only from the supplied material, and do not rely on the network. Choose a clear layout, readable text sizes and a consistent look, because a person will look at the deck.

You are the whole system for this task. You may use your own sub-agents, workers, or parallel sessions the way you normally would. Everything they do counts as your work and your cost. Do not get help from outside your own system: no human, and no other contestant.

## References

Everything you need is in `source.md`. No outside research is needed.

## Verification expected

Open `deck.pptx` yourself after you save it. Check the slide count, that every slide has content, that the notes are present, and that the chart or table shows the numbers from the source.

## Final response

State which file you wrote, how many slides it has, and what you checked. Say what you could not check. Do not claim the deck looks good unless you looked at it.
